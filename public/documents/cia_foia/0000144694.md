**MEMORANDUM FOR:** The Director

The lack of formal G-2 concurrence is, I believe, related to the existing confusion on all scientific intelligence matters on which you conferred with Gen. [redacted] et al., recently. The taking of the action here recommended is consistent with the "standstill" agreement reached at that meeting, since R&DB could act for whom it may concern when the final allocation of responsibility is made. I expressed myself to this effect to Gen. [redacted] and was assured on his behalf that G-2's recommendation in the JIC would be the same as that herein recommended, namely, that the assistance of R&DB be sought. Accordingly, I recommend proceeding without awaiting formal concurrence by G-2.

*(Initialed/Signed)*
