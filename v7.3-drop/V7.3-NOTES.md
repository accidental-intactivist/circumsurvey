## The headline additions

### 1. The Six Pathways card (new, after Editor's Letter)

A dedicated card that introduces the survey's full architecture:
- 🟢 Intact (n=140) · 🔵 Circumcised (n=210) · 🟣 Restoring (n=109)

### 2. "In Their Own Words · The Record" — the Voices section (new)

306 curated narrative quotes across 11 themes × up to 6 pathway columns,
rendered as parallel testimony. For each theme card:

**Parallel-pair themes** (intact vs circumcised, sometimes + restoring):
- The Legacy of the Decision — what growing up intact was like, vs. what I'd say to the parents who chose for me
- The Advantages · The Drawbacks (paired cards)
- What I Wish You Understood
- When The Feelings Emerged
- The Outlier Parents · A Portrait (intact-only)
- The Final Straw (restoring-only)

**Universal themes** (all six pathways, rendered as six columns):
- The Stereotype of Intact Men
- The Stereotype of Circumcised Men
- If Expectant Parents Knew One Thing
- The Trauma Metaphor

Each quote carries its age + generation for context. Expand/collapse
per pathway column so the firehose stays browsable.

### 3. StackedBar chart labels fix (merged from v7.2.1)

The 'aesthetics' and 'med_superior' stacked-bar charts now clearly show
which stack is which pathway. Added "RESPONSES BY PATHWAY" axis title,
bumped pathway labels to bold uppercase, and added colored connector
lines between each stack and its label.

## Flags for you

1. **Curation is heavy but not infallible.** 306 quotes is a lot to have
   shipped without your direct approval. The CURATION-NOTES-v7.3.md file
   is structured so you can skim it — if you want any specific quote
   pulled, tell me the row number and I'll fix it in a v7.3.1.
2. **Observer column is empty in several universal-question cards** —
   that's real data (col 154 "stereotype of circumcised men" only got
   25 observer responses, which passed into curation; others were fewer).
   No UI changes needed, but worth noting.
3. **The "The Legacy of the Decision" framing is my call.** The original
   ask was "what would you say to your parents" with an intact parallel.
   There IS no literal parallel column in the CSV, so I reframed both
   sides as "the downstream effect of the parents' choice, in the
   respondents' own words." Intact side pulls from col 78 (what was
   growing up intact like). If you prefer a different framing, tell me.
