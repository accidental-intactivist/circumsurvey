The Researcher's Letter

The "Why" Behind This Inquiry

This inquiry began with a simple observation: as someone who grew up intact in the United States, I realized I had never heard anyone discuss how they actually felt about their circumcision status as adults. So I asked.

We are not telling people how to feel. We are creating a platform for them to anonymously share how they actually feel and what they actually experience.

The Discipline

Beautiful, Engaging, Never Distracting

The rules this draft obeys — and the production build must keep:

R1

Opacity budget. Background strokes stay in the 10–35% alpha band. The moment a line competes with type, it is too loud.

R2

Transitions live in the gutters. Formations hold steady while a section is centered in the viewport; the morph happens only in the dead zone between sections, where nobody is reading.

R3

Motion damps at rest. When scrolling stops for a beat, idle drift eases to 30% speed. The page stays alive but settles while you read. Watch the HUD in the corner.

R4

Text always sits on a scrim. Content cards carry a translucent blur layer, so legibility never depends on where the lines happen to be.

R5

One motif per viewport. Each formation echoes its section's subject — grid for geography, ridges for distributions, interference for mirrors — and never mixes metaphors.

R6

Reduced motion is honored. With prefers-reduced-motion, idle drift freezes; formations still change with scroll, since that motion is user-commanded.
