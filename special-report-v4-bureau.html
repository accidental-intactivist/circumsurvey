★ Letter of Introduction
FORM CS-000 · READ FIRST
Why this inquiry exists
Nobody was asking. So I did.
By a conscious choice of my parents in the 1970s, I grew up intact in a culture where that made me the outlier. I noticed early that while everyone had an opinion about whether the procedure should happen, I almost never heard adults speak honestly about their own lived experience of it. This survey is my way of finally asking — respectfully, anonymously, with room for every perspective, including full satisfaction. I have opinions of my own; you can read them elsewhere. Here, the respondents do the talking, and the conclusions are yours to draw.
→ Signed: Tone Pettit · The Accidental Intactivist · Lead Researcher
→ Methodology, ethics & limitations: FORM CS-001, directly below

What this instrument can — and cannot — tell you
This is a self-selected sample, drawn largely from communities already thinking about this topic. It is not a census, and we never pretend otherwise. What it offers is rarer: the same structured questions answered from both sides of the anatomical fence — with the sexual-experience questions asked before any mention of status, so baseline answers were given before the comparison was framed.
