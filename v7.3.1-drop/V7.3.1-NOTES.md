# v7.3.1 — Card Summaries + Avg-Card Redundancy Fix

Single-file patch. Ships on top of v7.3.

## Fix 1: Avg-card redundant legend removed

On "Sexual Experience Assessment" cards (and any `type: "avg"` card), the
right-hand "MEAN RATING BY PATHWAY" list was duplicating the numbers
already shown on the bars themselves. Same three numbers, twice, with
only a short "Δ 1.39" note as the actually-useful bit.

The whole redundant block is replaced with a proper **editorial callout**
titled "The Finding" — a headline + explanatory body paragraph that
tells you what the data means, not what the numbers are.

## Fix 2: First-batch CARD_SUMMARIES for all six Pleasure Gap cards

- Intensity of Orgasm — the 25% gap, what it means
- Duration of Orgasm — the 26% gap, the "full point on a 5-point scale" framing
- Ease of Reaching Orgorgasm — the 15% gap, why even a smaller gap is notable
- Sensitivity to Light Touch — the 41% gap, the anatomy behind it
- Pleasure from Mobile Skin — the 36% gap, the largest mechanical loss
- Variety of Sensations — the 46% gap, why variety is hardest to restore

Each summary is 2-3 sentences of actual finding-ful interpretation, not
restated statistics.

## What's NOT in this drop

- **Non-avg cards still use the existing "Response Distribution" layout.**
  Only avg cards were affected. Adding summaries to more cards (Mirror,
  Aesthetics, Med-Superior, Autonomy, Future Son) is follow-up work — the
  `CARD_SUMMARIES` lookup table is now in place and extending it is a
  data-only change going forward.
- **Hello Tomorrow typography pass — still deferred.** I'd rather do this
  in a session where I can preview each card as I change it. My proposal
  when you're back: keep Playfair for hero/sections/findings, swap
  Josefin Sans + Outfit into the chrome (card titles, eyebrows, chips,
  Act-on-This labels), use #f09860 as a secondary accent, soften corners
  slightly. Not a full replacement — a Playfair/Josefin hybrid that keeps
  the editorial gravitas while modernizing the UI chrome.
