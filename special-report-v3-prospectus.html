A letter from the lead researcher

This inquiry began with a simple observation: as someone who grew up intact in the United States, I realized I had never heard anyone discuss how they actually felt about their circumcision status as adults.

So I asked — respectfully, anonymously, with room for every perspective. Five hundred people answered questions most of them had never been asked before. This prospectus is a guided tour through what they said. I have opinions of my own, and you can read them elsewhere; here, the respondents do the talking, and the conclusions are yours to draw.

Tone PettitThe Accidental Intactivist · Lead Researcher

Section One
Who Answered

Five hundred people, gathered anonymously, sorted by the survey into pathways — so that every question could be asked in the words that fit each life.

A note before the numbers: this is a self-selected sample, drawn largely from communities already thinking about the topic. It is not a census, and we never pretend otherwise. What it offers is rarer — the same structured questions, answered from both sides of the anatomical fence, with the sexual-experience questions asked before any mention of status.
