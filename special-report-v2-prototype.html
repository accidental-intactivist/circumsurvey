Memo · 01
From the Lead Researcher

A simple observation

This inquiry began with a simple observation: as someone who grew up intact in the United States, I realized I had never heard anyone discuss how they actually felt about their circumcision status as adults.

So I asked — respectfully, anonymously, and with room for every perspective. What follows is what five hundred people said when someone finally asked. I have opinions about this practice, and you can read them elsewhere. This report is not that. Here, the respondents speak, and you draw your own conclusions.

Tone PettitThe Accidental Intactivist · Lead Researcher

How the instrument works — and what it can’t tell you
We show you the caveats first, because that’s the honest order.

This is a self-selected sample recruited largely from communities already thinking about this topic. It is not a random population survey, and we never present it as one. What it is: one of the largest structured, comparative records of how people describe their own lived experience of their circumcision status — asked the same questions, in the same words, from both sides of the anatomical fence. The survey asked about sexual experience before asking about status, so baseline answers were given before the comparison was framed.
