# Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

**TO:** Chief, Technical Branch  
**DATE:** 17 April 1953  
**FROM:** [REDACTED] *(Margin Note: A)*  
**SUBJECT:** Recommendation Regarding Experimental Program in [REDACTED] *(Margin Note: B)*

1. Following the meeting of 16 April 1953 on the ARTICHOKE project, the following recommendations are submitted on the experimental program in [REDACTED] *(Margin Note: B)*

    (1) *(Margin Note: E)* After the clearance of [REDACTED] is obtained, contact [REDACTED] to definitely ascertain whether they are interested in working on a project in which the Agency is interested if mutually satisfactory terms of definite research objectives and payment, etc. are arranged. I am morally certain that these two individuals will be interested in such a project as their general interests have been determined and they are definitely interested in this particular area.

    (2) *(Margin Note: C)* Assuming that [REDACTED] are interested in pursuing the project further, I suggest that the undersigned go to [REDACTED] *(Margin Note: B)* to definitely determine if these men are interested in becoming covert consultants to the Agency; whether they would be interested in working on research projects as individuals in their own organization or outside or whether they would prefer to work as a team; whether they prefer financing through a fund organization or whether funds could be directly given to them and other definite suggestions that they may have as to the procedure of such a project.

    (3) After this discussion, a meeting should be held in Washington to determine whether the Agency approves the proposals and recommendations made by the undersigned and the three prospective consultants. If this approval is forthcoming as I assume it will, *(Margin Note: (D))* then the next steps can be taken to make these people Agency consultants.

    (4) Assuming that the three aforementioned people become consultants of the Agency, a meeting will be arranged either in [REDACTED] *(Margin Note: B)* or Washington to set up tentative research plans. At this time, decisions can be made
