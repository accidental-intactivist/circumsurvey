STANDARD FORM NO. 64

# Office Memorandum • UNITED STATES GOVERNMENT

**TO:** Assistant Director, Scientific Intelligence  
**DATE:** 20 May 1952  
**FROM:** Deputy Security Officer/CIA  
**SUBJECT:** Special Interrogations.  
**REFERENCE:** TS [REDACTED] dated 15 May 1952, Staff Study on Special Interrogations (Project ARTICHOKE).

1. This Office concurs in the Staff Study dated 15 May 1952 to the DD/CI on special interrogations, Project ARTICHOKE.

2. This Office will assume, upon appropriate approval, the responsibilities placed on it in the Staff Study and at the expiration of the six months period a meeting will be called to determine the results achieved during the interim six months period and determination made as to whether I&SO or some other office should assume permanent responsibility for the conduct of this Project.

[REDACTED SIGNATURE]
